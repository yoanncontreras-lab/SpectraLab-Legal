# SpectraLab Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 5, 2026_

SpectraLab is a paranormal investigation and evidence-analysis application published by DAEDALUS.

## 1. Data processed by the application

Depending on the features you use, SpectraLab may access or process:
- microphone audio;
- camera images and video;
- device sensors and motion data;
- files you choose to import or export;
- locally stored investigation notes, evidence, and reports;
- Google Play purchase and subscription status;
- advertising-related data handled by Google AdMob when advertising is displayed.

## 2. Local-first processing

SpectraLab is designed to operate primarily on-device. Investigation data, evidence, notes, analysis results, and generated reports are stored locally unless you explicitly choose to export, share, or save them elsewhere.

SpectraLab does not require a DAEDALUS account or a mandatory SpectraLab cloud service for its core operation.

## 3. Permissions

SpectraLab may request access to the microphone, camera, storage/files, motion sensors, nearby-device capabilities, or other Android permissions where required by a feature.

Permissions are used only to provide the corresponding feature and can be managed from Android system settings.

## 4. Advertising

The free version of SpectraLab may use Google AdMob to display rewarded advertising. Advertising is not intended to interrupt an active investigation.

Google and its advertising partners may process device identifiers, approximate location, diagnostics, advertising interactions, and related data in accordance with Google's own privacy policies and consent requirements.

## 5. Purchases and subscriptions

SpectraLab may offer Google Play subscriptions and promotional access to Pro features.

Payment information is processed by Google Play. DAEDALUS does not receive or store your full payment-card details.

## 6. Pro and partner access

SpectraLab may support signed Pro, tester, owner, or partner access passes. These passes are used to determine feature access. They are not intended to contain sensitive personal information.

## 7. Sharing and exports

SpectraLab may let you export or share audio, video, images, reports, or evidence packages. Any destination or recipient you select is under your control.

## 8. Children

SpectraLab is not designed or marketed as a children's application.

## 9. Data retention

Locally stored data remains on your device until you delete it, reset the application, uninstall the application, or remove exported files from their destination.

Data processed by Google Play or Google AdMob is subject to Google's own retention practices.

## 10. Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect application data and Pro-access verification. However, no software or storage method can guarantee absolute security.

## 11. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when SpectraLab features, legal requirements, or third-party services change. The current version will remain available at this public URL.
